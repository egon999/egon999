### ⚡ Executive Overview

I am a **Systems Architect & Performance Engineer** and the founder of **Bratz Inc.** My engineering focus centers on low-level OS internals, sub-millisecond execution latency, anti-tamper security, and competitive digital forensics.

- 🏢 **Founder & Lead Developer**: [Bratz Inc.](https://discord.gg/dtAu3McPhY)
- 🛡️ **Flagship Project**: **ATLAS by Bratz** — Enterprise Windows performance engineering & reverse-engineering threat hunter.
- 🔬 **Core Competencies**: Win32 / NT APIs, kernel driver interop, thread injection detection, raw UTF-16LE ShimCache/AppCompatCache forensics, and memory analysis.
- ⚡ **Engineering Philosophy**: Maximum hardware efficiency, zero-bloat architectures, and deep defensive security.
